# Driver Arriving Notifications

## Problem
Customers contact support when they do not know how close the driver is (source: LMD contact driver report).

## Proposal
Send live tracking push notifications when the driver is five minutes away. This will cut where-is-my-order contacts by 35%.

## Success Metrics
- Contact Rate: baseline 8.4 per 100, target 7.0 per 100 within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: stop if it falls by more than 0.05.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by customer. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 0.4 contacts per 100, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Notification platform team has capacity.
