# Entrance Learning From Support Chats

## Problem
Drivers lose time finding building entrances for apartment customers (source: LMD driver feedback survey).

## Proposal
Mine the coordinates customers share in support chats and store them against the customer profile, so the driver app can show the right entrance next time.

## Success Metrics
- Promise Accuracy, median error: baseline 6.8 minutes, target 5.5 minutes within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: stop if it falls by more than 0.05.
- Contact Rate: stop if it rises by more than 0.3 per 100.
- Promise Accuracy P90: must not worsen.

## Experiment Plan
Geo-level randomization across matched markets. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 0.5 minutes, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.
