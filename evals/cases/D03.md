# Pickup Readiness Indicator In Driver App

## Problem
Drivers wait at store pickup because they cannot see when an order is ready, which wastes time and delays customers.

## Proposal
Show a ready-for-pickup status in the driver app, fed by store staging events, so drivers time their arrival.

## Success Metrics
- Delivery success: from 91% to 95% within 8 weeks.
- Customer delight: improve survey score from 4.1 to 4.4 by end of Q1.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: stop if it rises by more than $0.10 over baseline.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by driver. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 1 point, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Depends on the store staging event feed owned by the store systems team.
