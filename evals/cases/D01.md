# Proactive Delay Notification

## Problem
Customers whose orders run late contact support to ask where the order is. Contact Rate on late orders was 14.2 per 100 in the last quarter (source: LMD weekly dashboard).

## Proposal
When the predicted arrival slips more than 10 minutes past the promised time, send one notification with a refreshed arrival estimate. Cap at two pushes per order, following the fatigue learning in EXP-035.

## Success Metrics
- Contact Rate on late orders: baseline 14.2 per 100, target 12.0 per 100 within 8 weeks of launch.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: baseline 4.31, stop if it falls by more than 0.05.
- Contact Rate on all orders: stop if it rises by more than 0.3 per 100.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by customer. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 1.0 contact per 100 late orders, power 80%, sample size computed by Data Science. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered; no early stopping on a win.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Uses existing order status events only. Engineering estimate attached in the planning ticket.
