# Pickup Ready Indicator, Driver Experience

## Problem
Drivers report frustration when orders are not ready at pickup.

## Proposal
Add a ready indicator in the driver app showing staging status for each order.

## Success Metrics
- Driver happiness: improve.
- Faster handoffs: improve.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: must not increase.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by store cluster. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 1 point, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Store systems team to provide the event feed.
