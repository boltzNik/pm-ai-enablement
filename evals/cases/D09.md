# Dispatch Priority For Nearby Drivers

## Problem
Offers sometimes go to drivers who are farther away than available closer drivers (source: LMD dispatch audit report).

## Proposal
Change the dispatch assignment algorithm to prefer the nearest available driver with a small tie-break on rating.

## Success Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: baseline 87%, target 89% within 6 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery Defect Rate: stop if it rises by more than 0.2 points.
- Driver Safety Incident Rate: stop if it rises more than 5% over baseline.

## Experiment Plan
A/B test randomized by driver. Run for 5 days. Compare on-time rate between arms.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Dispatch engineering has capacity next sprint.
