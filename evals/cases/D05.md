# Zone-Aware Batching For Suburban Zones

## Problem
Orders per trip stay low in suburban zones, which keeps cost high (source: LMD zone efficiency report).

## Proposal
Allow batching of up to two orders per trip in suburban zones only, building on EXP-027 learnings about dense zones.

## Success Metrics
- Orders Per Trip: baseline 1.12, target 1.35 within 10 weeks.
- Cost Per Delivery: from $7.40 to $7.10 within 10 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: must not fall below baseline.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized at zone level. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 0.1 orders per trip, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Routing team capacity confirmed for the next sprint.
