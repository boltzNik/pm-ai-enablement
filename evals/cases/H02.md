# Tracking Page Redesign

## Problem
Customers open the tracking page but many still contact support, suggesting the page does not answer their question (source: LMD contact driver report).

## Proposal
Redesign the tracking page to show a clearer arrival range and driver progress, reusing the cap and learnings from EXP-035.

## Success Metrics
- Contact Rate: baseline 8.4 per 100, target 7.6 per 100 within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: stop if it falls by more than 0.05.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by customer. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 0.4 contacts per 100, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Design review done; no regulated data involved.
