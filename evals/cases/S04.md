# Proactive Calls For Late Orders

## Problem
Late deliveries are the single biggest reason customers churn, and fixing them would save the business millions every year.

## Proposal
Operations agents phone customers whose orders are running late to explain the delay and offer a reschedule.

## Success Metrics
- Delivery CSAT on late orders: baseline 3.6, target 3.9 within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: stop if it rises by more than $0.15.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by customer. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 0.15 CSAT points, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Contact center has agreed to staff a pilot team.
