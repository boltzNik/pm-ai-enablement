# Smaller Dispatch Radius In Dense Zones

## Problem
In dense zones drivers are offered orders from farther away than needed, adding pickup time (source: LMD dispatch audit report).

## Proposal
Reduce the dispatch search radius in dense zones from the current setting to the next smaller tier.

## Success Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: baseline 89%, target 91% within 6 weeks.
- Cost Per Delivery: from $7.40 to $7.20 within 6 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery Defect Rate: stop if it rises by more than 0.2 points.
- Driver Safety Incident Rate: stop if it rises more than 5% over baseline.

## Experiment Plan
Switchback by zone-day. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 1 point of on-time rate, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Dispatch engineering and Data Science have signed off on the design.
