# Evening Peak Incentive, Narrow Pilot

## Problem
Weekday evening acceptance dips versus midday (source: LMD driver supply dashboard, weeks 30-38), leaving orders unassigned and late.

## Proposal
A time-boxed evening bonus in three markets, built on the learning in EXP-021 that zone-level tests overstate lift because neighboring zones lose drivers. We test at market level with a switchback and track neighbors explicitly.

## Success Metrics
- Driver Acceptance Rate, 5-8pm weekdays: baseline 71%, target 77% within 6 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Driver Hourly Earnings: must not fall below baseline in any arm.
- Driver Safety Incident Rate: stop if it rises more than 5% over baseline.
- Cost Per Delivery: stop if it rises more than $0.45.

## Experiment Plan
Switchback by market-day. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 2 points of acceptance, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered with Data Science.
