# Choice Of Two Arrival Ranges At Checkout

## Problem
Customers with flexible schedules cannot trade a later arrival for a cheaper or more reliable slot (source: LMD checkout survey).

## Proposal
Let customers pick between two delivery windows at checkout, with the later window shown as more reliable.

## Success Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: baseline 88%, target 90% within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Average customer rating after delivery: stop if it falls by more than 0.05.
- Support contacts per 100 orders: stop if it rises by more than 0.3.
- Median arrival-time error: must not worsen.

## Experiment Plan
Switchback by market-day. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 1 point of on-time rate, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Checkout team has capacity next sprint.
