# Referral Bonus For New Drivers

## Problem
New driver supply is short in growing markets (source: LMD driver supply dashboard).

## Proposal
Offer a referral bonus to existing drivers who bring in a new driver who completes their first fifty deliveries.

## Success Metrics
- Driver Acceptance Rate: baseline 71%, target 75% within 10 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Driver Hourly Earnings: must not fall below baseline.
- Driver Safety Incident Rate: stop if it rises more than 5% over baseline.
- Cost Per Delivery: stop if it rises more than $0.20.

## Experiment Plan
Switchback by market-week. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 2 points of acceptance, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Legal review: waived per VP email, no ticket.
