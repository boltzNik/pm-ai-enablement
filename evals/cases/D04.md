# Store Staging Zones For Pickup

## Problem
Orders are staged inconsistently at stores, which slows pickup and contributes to late deliveries.

## Proposal
Create labeled staging zones in pilot stores and train store associates to place delivery orders there.

## Success Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: improve.
- Cost Per Delivery: reduce.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: must not decline.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by store. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 1 point of on-time rate, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Store operations has agreed to a 20-store pilot.
