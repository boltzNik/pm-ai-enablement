# Ops Dashboard For Existing Surge Spend

## Problem
Operations cannot see surge spend by market without asking an analyst (source: LMD ops interview notes).

## Proposal
Build a read-only dashboard of existing surge spend by market and hour. It does not change pay rate, incentive rules, or any driver-facing behavior.

## Success Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: baseline $7.40, target $7.25 within 12 weeks through faster detection of overspend.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: reported weekly for all markets, no decision rule changes.

## Experiment Plan
No experiment: read-only dashboard with no user-facing change. Adoption tracked from usage logs.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Data access follows the existing analyst permission model.
