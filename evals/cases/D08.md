# Automatic Credit For Very Late Deliveries

## Problem
Industry data shows that 12% of customers never reorder after a late delivery, costing roughly $48M a year.

## Proposal
Automatically apply a fixed-value credit to the next order when a delivery arrives more than 30 minutes late.

## Success Metrics
- Delivery CSAT on late orders: baseline 3.6, target 3.9 within 8 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Cost Per Delivery: stop if it rises by more than $0.15.

## Experiment Plan
Randomized by customer. Duration: 3 weeks. MDE: 0.15 CSAT points, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
Finance has been informed of the expected credit volume.
