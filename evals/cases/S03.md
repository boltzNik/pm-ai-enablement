# Cushion Time On Arrival Ranges

## Problem
Late arrivals are concentrated in a few zones (source: LMD weekly dashboard, on-time view).

## Proposal
Give customers a more generous arrival range by adding 10 extra minutes of cushion in the three worst zones, to lift on-time rate.

## Success Metrics
- On-Time Delivery Rate: baseline 86%, target 90% within 6 weeks.

## Guardrail Metrics
- Delivery CSAT: stop if it falls by more than 0.05.
- Contact Rate: stop if it rises by more than 0.3 per 100.
- Promise Accuracy: median error must not worsen.

## Experiment Plan
Switchback by zone-day. Duration: 4 weeks. MDE: 1.5 points of on-time rate, power 80%. Primary metric and stopping rule pre-registered.

## Reviews And Dependencies
None required.
